About Zaheer Seed Corporation

Zaheer Seed Corporation is an agricultural company based in Kasur, Pakistan. Our work covers crop seed breeding, processing and distribution.

Seed is the first input of every season, and the quality of what goes into the ground shapes everything that follows. Our work is to breed it carefully, prepare it properly and see it reach growers in good condition.

We serve farmers and dealers directly from our office in Kasur, and we welcome questions about the seed we offer.

Our Process

From the first selection to the sack in a grower’s hands, our work follows four stages.

Step 1

Seed Breeding

We develop and select crop seed with the needs of local growers in mind, choosing plants that perform well season after season.

Step 2

Seed Processing

Harvested seed is cleaned, sorted and prepared so that each lot is uniform and ready for packing.

Step 3

Quality Handling

Careful storage and handling protect seed from damage and moisture between processing and sale.

Step 4

Distribution

Prepared seed is supplied to dealers and farmers, with our team available to answer questions along the way.
